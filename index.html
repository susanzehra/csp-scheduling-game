Real-world scenario

Staff the four call-center shifts

A call center has four employees—A, B, C, and D—and four daily shifts. Assign each employee to exactly one shift while obeying all rules.

Employees→Rules→Schedule
Constraints

Rules to satisfy

  • ○Use each employee exactly once.
  • ○Employee A cannot work Shift 4.
  • ○Employee B must work Shift 1 or Shift 2.
  • ○Employee C cannot work Shift 1.
  • ○Employee D must work a later shift than Employee A.
CSP idea: employees are variables, shifts are domain values, and the rules are constraints.
