Real-world scenario

Staff the five call-center shifts

A call center has five employees—A, B, C, D, and E—and five daily shifts. Assign each employee to exactly one shift while obeying all rules. Several rules depend on one another, so test your choices carefully.

Employees→Rules→Schedule
Constraints

Rules to satisfy

  • ○Use each employee exactly once.
  • ○Employee A must work a middle shift—Shift 2, 3, or 4.
  • ○Employee C must work immediately before Employee A.
  • ○Employee D must work earlier than Employee C.
  • ○Employee B must work later than Employee A.
  • ○Employee E must work earlier than Employee B.
  • ○Employees D and E cannot work adjacent shifts.
CSP idea: employees are variables, shifts are domain values, and the rules are constraints.

Question 2 • Map coloring

Color the regions of Australia

A map designer needs to color Australia so neighboring regions are easy to distinguish. Assign one of three colors to every region. Regions that share a land border must have different colors.

Regions→Border rules→Valid map
Constraints

Map rules

  • ○Color all seven regions.
  • ○Use only red, green, or blue.
  • ○Regions sharing a land border cannot use the same color.
  • ○Tasmania has no land border with another region, so it may use any color.
CSP idea: regions are variables, colors are domain values, and shared borders create constraints.

Color the map
